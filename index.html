01

Data controller and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how data is processed in the mobile game Project Rival. The data controller is:

DATA CONTROLLERNullForgePolandnullforgesupport@gmail.com

This Policy covers data processed during registration, sign-in, use of online features, gameplay, and the display of advertisements.

02

Data we process

Depending on the game features you use, we may process the following categories of data:

01

Account and profile

Email address or other sign-in information, account identifier, and player name.

02

Gameplay data

Progress, statistics, inventory, and purchased or unlocked items assigned to the account. We do not store payment card details or Google Play transaction records.

03

Diagnostics and device

App and operating system version, device model, crash reports, and data needed to keep the game stable.

04

Advertising

Advertising identifier and data related to delivering and measuring non-personalized ads. Project Rival does not use this data to personalize ads.

We do not sell personal data. The exact scope of processing also depends on your device settings and how you use the game.

Age requirement: Project Rival is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

03

How we use data

  • creating and securing accounts and managing sign-in;
  • saving progress, statistics, and purchased or unlocked items assigned to a profile;
  • providing online gameplay and data synchronization;
  • detecting abuse, fraud, and violations of game rules;
  • diagnosing errors and improving stability and performance;
  • serving, frequency-capping, and measuring non-personalized advertisements.

We process data to provide the service, based on consent where required, to comply with legal obligations, or for our legitimate interests in protecting and improving the game.

04

Service providers and data recipients

Project Rival uses technical services needed to operate the game. Data may be processed by providers of infrastructure, authentication, online gameplay, diagnostics, and advertising, including:

Google Firebaseaccounts, database, and technical services
Photonnetworking and multiplayer features
Unity / Unity Adsgame engine, diagnostics, and advertising

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, legally required transfer safeguards are applied.

05

Data retention and deletion

Data assigned to your account is retained while you use Project Rival. Once you confirm “Delete Account”, your account and all associated data are permanently deleted immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Fully anonymized information that can no longer be linked to a user is not personal data. Transaction records stored independently by Google Play are governed by that platform's own policies.

06

Your rights

Where provided by applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, or data portability. You may also object to processing, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with the appropriate data protection authority.

The fastest way to delete your data is through the option available directly in the game settings. Other privacy requests can be sent to nullforgesupport@gmail.com.

07

Security and policy changes

We use appropriate organizational and technical measures, including access controls and encrypted transmission, to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. However, no method of transmission or storage can guarantee the complete absence of risk.

We may update this Policy when game features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will always be available on this page.

Account controls

Delete Your Account

Permanently delete your Project Rival account and all associated data.

DIRECTLY IN THE GAME
  1. 1
    Launch Project Rival

    Sign in to the account you want to delete.

  2. 2
    Open Settings

    From the game menu, open the account settings section.

  3. 3
    Select “Delete Account”

    Open the Account tab and choose the deletion option.

  4. 4
    Confirm deletion

    Once confirmed, the account and its data are deleted immediately.

!

Account deletion is permanent

After confirmation, your account and progress cannot be recovered.

We delete:
  • account and sign-in identifier
  • player name and profile
  • progress, statistics, and inventory
  • purchased or unlocked items assigned to the account
  • linked technical and diagnostic data
●   Deletion is immediate
NO ACCESS TO THE GAME?

Request deletion by email

Contact us from the email address assigned to your account and include the account email and your in-game player name. Never include your password. We may request limited information needed to verify ownership before deleting the account.
